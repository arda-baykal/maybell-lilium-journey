A note on terminology

The diagram uses two distinct terms: Interview for the meeting before tenancy is signed, and Intake for the day a resident signs the tenancy agreement and moves in. We'd like to walk through this with you in person on our next call.

For reference

  • Wait-list reality: 6 months to 1.5 years between short-form and a unit opening. Long-form is requested only when capacity is realistic.
  • Two consents, not one: a general release embedded in the long-form, plus a secondary consent (e.g. child-protection releases) signed at move-in.
  • 12 units · 6 duplex buildings · 3 accessible (units 1, 7, 10).
  • Two users today, same access level.
Outcome indicators come from MayBell's logic model (still in draft). The build will use Michelle's stated indicators — relationships built, graduations from programming, program participation — and absorb the final logic model when it lands.

Questions for your team to think through

These are the spots where we need your direction before we start configuring. No right answers — just pick what feels right for how your team actually works. If you're unsure, flag it and we'll talk through it on the next call.

  1. Community partners. What are the names of the three recurring community-partner organizations you mentioned? We'd like to seed the partner directory with them and track which cases they're connected to.
  2. Encouragement Program — rhythm. Is the program mandatory for every resident? Is it on a fixed rotation across the year, or does each resident enrol when they're ready? And what does "graduating" from it mean concretely?
  3. Transition Out. What does success at exit look like? Are there exit-stage measures or a structured close-out conversation, or is it case-by-case today?
  4. Logic model. When can we expect the draft? Even an early version helps us shape the outcome-indicator structure so we don't have to rework it later.
  5. Both consent forms. Could you share the long-form embedded consent and the secondary consent so we can model what each one unlocks (information sharing, partner releases, child-protection releases)?
  6. Priority statuses. Domestic violence, exiting human trafficking, currently or at risk of homelessness — is this list exhaustive, or are there others to design fields for?
  7. Wait-list ordering. When a unit opens, is the next resident chosen by priority status alone, by how long they've been waiting, by a judgement call combining both, or something else?
  8. Accessibility units. Units 1, 7, and 10 are the larger accessible ones. Should Mareto enforce assignment by accessibility need, or just suggest it and let staff decide?
  9. Board reporting cadence. How often does the board report go out, and what sections does it usually contain? We'd like the dashboard to fold cleanly into your existing Word template rather than replacing it.
